Lunaria

A Little Garden of Light

Welcome to Lunaria — a tiny garden where flowers bloom beneath the moonlight and little moments turn into magic.

A quiet little space created to slow down, explore, and enjoy the simple beauty of nature. Whether it's midnight or daylight, there's always something waiting to bloom here.

✧

How to Explore

☀

Change the Light

Tap the moon or sun button in the top-right corner to switch between Midnight Garden and Sunlit Garden.

🌸

Wake the Flowers

Click or tap any flower to make it glow. Tap it again to let it rest.

✨

Follow the Magic

Watch the tiny fireflies dance around the garden and enjoy the little details.

"Every little light has a story."
